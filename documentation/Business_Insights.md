The Bank Marketing campaign achieved an overall term-deposit subscription rate of 11.70%, with 5,289 out of 45,211 clients subscribing. 



Cellular was the most effective major contact method, achieving a 14.92% subscription rate. 



Previous campaign success was the strongest observed indicator of future subscription, with a 64.73% subscription rate among previously successful clients. 



Customers aged 60+, students, retired clients, tertiary-educated customers, and customers without housing or personal loans showed comparatively higher subscription rates. 



Successful customers also had longer average contact durations, while unsuccessful customers received more campaign contacts on average. 



These findings suggest that the bank should prioritize targeted customer segmentation, previous successful responders, effective contact channels, and optimized contact frequency rather than applying the same strategy to all customers.
